How to read this

Last 7 days

The rain that fell in the 7 days to the date at the top, at the district's main town.

Normal

The middle value for the same week of the year (give or take a week) in every year from 2015.

Wetter than

The share of those past weeks that were drier. 97% means only 3 in 100 comparable weeks were wetter.

Levels

Much wetter: wetter than 95% of past weeks and at least 50 mm. Wetter: wetter than 80% and at least 25 mm. Much drier: drier than 95% of past weeks, at a time of year that is usually wet.

Forecast

Heavy rain likely means 100 mm or more forecast on one of the next 3 days, and fairly heavy 75 mm or more, the Department of Meteorology's wording.

This is not an official warning. For warnings, follow the Department of Meteorology and the Disaster Management Centre.

One point per district stands for the whole district, and rainfall here comes from weather models, not rain gauges.
